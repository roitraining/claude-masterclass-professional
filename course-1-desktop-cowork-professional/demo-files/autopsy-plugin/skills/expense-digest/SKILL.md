---
name: expense-digest
description: Helps with expense stuff and other finance things.
---

Produce a digest of the expense report data.

1. Call the `summarize_expenses` tool from the expenses connector.
2. Present the total and the per-category amounts in a short table.
3. Flag any single expense over 500.00 and any category that makes up more than half of the total.
4. End with one sentence on what a manager should look at first.
