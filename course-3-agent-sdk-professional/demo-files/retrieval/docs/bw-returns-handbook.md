# Returns Handbook (current)

Status: current. Effective 2026-01-01.

Customers may return items within 30 days of the delivery date. Items must be unused and in original packaging. Refunds go to the original payment method within 7 business days after the returned item is received.

Final sale items cannot be returned. Tents and sleeping bags must come back with their stuff sacks and all stakes or ties.

To start a return, the customer gives the order id. Support confirms the delivery date and sends a prepaid label. Exchanges follow the same 30-day window.
