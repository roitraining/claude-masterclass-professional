# Shipping Zones and Times

Status: current.

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. Standard shipping is free on orders of 75.00 or more.

Zone A covers the home region and usually arrives in 3 days. Zone B covers neighboring regions and usually arrives in 4 days. Zone C covers remote areas and usually arrives in 5 days. Weather holds at a regional hub can add 2 to 4 days, and support will message affected customers.
