# Warranty Guide (current)

Status: current.

Tents and sleeping bags carry a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects such as failed seams, broken zippers and delaminated coatings. Stoves and headlamps carry a 1-year warranty. All other items carry a 90-day warranty.

The warranty does not cover wear from normal use, damage from fire or misuse, or fading from sun exposure.

To claim, send the order id and two photos of the defect to the warranty desk. The desk replies within 3 business days with a repair, replacement or refund decision.
