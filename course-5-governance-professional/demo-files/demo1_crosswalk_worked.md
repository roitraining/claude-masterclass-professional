# Demo 1 Sample: One Control Across Two Frameworks

This is a teaching crosswalk built for this class. It is not an official NIST or ISO mapping. It works at the function level for NIST and at the clause level for ISO/IEC 42001. Do not cite subcategory numbers or Annex A control numbers from this page.

## Part A: Worked control

**Control:** Every AI use at Brightwater is recorded in an inventory with a named owner, a risk tier and a review date.

| Framework location | How the control lands there | What an assessor asks |
|---|---|---|
| NIST Govern | The policy requires the inventory and assigns who keeps it. | Show me the policy and the role accountable for the inventory. |
| NIST Map | Each inventory entry records the use, its data, and who is affected. | Show me an entry. How did you find uses nobody told you about? |
| NIST Measure | A coverage metric: share of uses with a current owner and review date, tested by sampling. | How do you know the inventory is complete? |
| NIST Manage | A use with no owner is escalated, given an owner by a deadline, or switched off. | What happened to the last orphaned use? |
| ISO/IEC 42001 clause 4 | The inventory defines the scope of the management system and who is affected. | What is in scope, and why? |
| ISO/IEC 42001 clause 5 | Top management assigns the inventory owner and signs the policy. | Who in top management is accountable? |
| ISO/IEC 42001 clause 6 | Inventory entries feed the risk assessment and set priorities. | How do the risks you rated trace to real uses? |
| ISO/IEC 42001 clause 7 | The inventory is documented information, kept current with trained people. | Who maintains it, and how are they trained? |
| ISO/IEC 42001 clause 8 | Intake runs the routing that adds, changes and retires entries. | Show an intake decision from last quarter. |
| ISO/IEC 42001 clause 9.2 | Internal audit samples the inventory against reality. | Show the last internal audit and its findings. |
| ISO/IEC 42001 clause 9.3 | Management review looks at inventory coverage and decides actions. | Show the minutes where leadership reviewed it. |
| ISO/IEC 42001 clause 10 | A missing entry is a finding. A corrective action closes it. | Show a finding and how you fixed the cause. |

**Evidence that answers the questions:** E08 (inventory snapshot), E07 (intake records), E14 (internal audit report), E15 (management review record). Names are in `evidence_menu.md`.

**EU AI Act overlay (awareness only):** the same inventory is the list counsel needs to judge which uses might fall in a regulated category. No inventory means no way to ask.

## What you should notice

- A single control lands in every function and in most clauses. A gap in the control shows up in several places at once.
- NIST tells you what kind of risk work to do. ISO/IEC 42001 tells an outside party what to audit.
