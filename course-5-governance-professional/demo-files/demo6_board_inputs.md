# Demo 6 Sample: Facts for the Board Report

Quarter ending August. All figures are synthetic. A strong report uses some of these facts and leaves the rest out. Decide which, and say why.

## The pile

1. 312 of 400 licensed seats are active weekly (78 percent).
2. Total prompts reached 51,300 in August, up from 31,200 in March.
3. The inventory holds 31 uses: 11 Tier 1, 13 Tier 2, 7 Tier 3.
4. Of the 7 Tier 3 uses, 2 have current evidence on file.
5. 23 exceptions are open. 11 are past their expiry date.
6. 2 incidents were reported: a Support agent pasted a ticket into an unapproved extension, and a Sales rep used a personal chatbot with a contract excerpt. Both are open.
7. Engineering's average time to produce a customer-facing change fell from 9 days to 6 days, measured from the ticket system.
8. Support's average ticket handling time is unchanged at 14 minutes.
9. The Governance Board met 3 times. Intake decisions took a median of 2 days for Tier 2 and 17 days for Tier 3 (target 10).
10. 94 percent of pilot seat holders completed the policy training.
11. The Halvern 30-day notice has not been sent. The Halvern renewal closes in five weeks.
12. The Dublin employee representatives have not yet been consulted on the meeting transcription assistant.
13. The CISO's team added a new dashboard widget showing prompts by hour.
14. An internal audit of the intake process has not been scheduled.
15. Seven vendor AI features switched on by default were found in a procurement review. Four are in the inventory.

## Tasks

1. Choose the status color and write the one-line reason.
2. Choose the decision to request of the board.
3. Choose the 4 risk posture measures.
4. Choose 2 value measures from what is here, or say none qualify and state what you would measure.
5. Name the one thing that could surprise the board.
6. Cut to one page.
