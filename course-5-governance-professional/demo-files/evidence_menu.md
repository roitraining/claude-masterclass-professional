# Evidence Menu

Evidence is a record an assessor can ask for and you can hand over. A commitment with no evidence is a promise, not a control. Cite evidence by its ID. A commitment may name several.

## Rules for good evidence

1. It is dated. An undated record proves nothing.
2. It has an owner who can produce it within a day.
3. It shows the control working, not only that a rule exists. A policy page is not evidence of a control.
4. It exists now, or the commitment names the date it will.
