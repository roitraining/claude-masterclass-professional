# Lab 1 Scoring Rubric

Total 100 points. A facilitator applies it by hand in about 10 minutes per policy.

## Part A: Gap closure (60 points)

Score each gap in `lab1_gap_check_sheet.md`.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Fixed. Passes the test, names a role, and states the mechanism. |
| 3 | Partly fixed. Improved, but a name, a date or a record is missing. |
| 0 | Open, or the fix creates a new gap. |

12 gaps, 5 points each.

## Part B: Fit to the organization (20 points)

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Resolves the speed versus legal conflict: Tier 1 and Tier 2 move fast, Tier 3 gets review, and the decision times are stated | 8 |
| Uses the facts correctly: data classes, Dublin and EU staff, Halvern notice, contractors, personal phones | 6 |
| Each rule points to a control that exists (identity and roles, Compliance API activity data, Tenant Restrictions, managed settings, spend limits) or to a record a person keeps | 6 |

## Part C: Employee test (20 points)

Hand the policy to a person who did not write it. Give them five minutes and these questions. They answer from the text alone. 4 points each, for a correct answer found without help.

1. A Sales rep wants to paste part of Halvern's contract into a free chatbot on a personal phone. Allowed? If not, what should the rep do instead?
2. A Support agent finds a browser extension that summarizes tickets. May they install it? Who do they ask, and how long should they wait?
3. A department head wants to keep using an unapproved tool for a month while a vendor review finishes. What must happen first, and when does it end?
4. An HR coordinator pasted a candidate's file into the wrong tool. Who do they tell, and what happens to them?
5. Who owns the policy, and when is it next reviewed?

## Bands

| Band | Score | Also required |
|---|---|---|
| Gold | 85 to 100 | No zero on G1, G2 or G5 |
| Silver | 70 to 84 | No zero on G1, G2 or G5 |
| Bronze | 50 to 69 | |
| Not yet | Below 50 | |

A zero on G1 (no named approver), G2 (exception with no expiry) or G5 (no consequence) caps the band at Bronze. These are the gaps assessors find first.

## Tie-break and scoring notes

- Award the points for what the draft says, not what the author meant.
- A rule that depends on a capability the policy cannot reach (for example, blocking a personal phone off the company network) scores 0 on that rule's check in Part B.
- Wording like "as soon as possible" and "where appropriate" scores 0 on the item it was meant to fix.
