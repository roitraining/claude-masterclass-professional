# Lab 2 Brief: Constraints and the Steering Committee Draft

Setting: six weeks after Lab 1's policy was approved. Dana Whitfield has asked for a governance program the leadership team can approve on Friday. A steering committee has drafted commitments. You are the program designer.

Read `brightwater_org_brief.md` first.

## Five constraints that conflict

| Ref | Constraint | Held by |
|---|---|---|
| K1 | No approval step may take longer than 2 business days. | Marcus Oyelaran, CRO |
| K2 | Legal signs off on every use that touches customer or employee data. Legal has 22 staff and no spare capacity. | Priya Raman, General Counsel |
| K3 | Block unapproved AI tools, and give every approved tool an owner. | Tomas Lindqvist, CISO |
| K4 | The program has 0.5 of a full-time person and no new headcount. | Elena Rossi, CFO |
| K5 | Staff monitoring needs prior consultation with Dublin employee representatives. The Audit Committee wants a written report every quarter. | Ciaran Doyle and Margaret Sloan |

Customer obligation, not negotiable: Halvern's 30-day notice and 30-day audit rights.

## The steering committee's draft commitments

| Ref | Draft commitment |
|---|---|
| C1 | The CISO chairs the AI Governance Board, approves every use of every tier, and conducts the quarterly audit. |
| C2 | Every AI use, of any kind, gets legal review within 2 business days. |
| C3 | All employee prompts are reviewed monthly for policy violations. |
| C4 | Success means 90 percent of staff use AI every week. |
| C5 | Exceptions are reviewed regularly. |
| C6 | All unapproved AI tools are blocked company-wide. |
| C7 | The program is fully funded within the existing budget and also runs a monthly audit of all tiers. |

| C9 | Spend is managed by Finance. |
| C10 | The board is updated as needed. |

## Your tasks

1. Audit the draft. For each commitment, name what is wrong.
2. Design the program using `lab2_program_design_template.md`.
3. Resolve every constraint conflict, and say who accepted what.
4. Tie every commitment to evidence from `evidence_menu.md`.
