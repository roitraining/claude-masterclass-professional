# Lab 2 Scoring Rubric

Total 100 points. The facilitator scores the program in about 10 minutes. The heaviest section is the evidence tie: every commitment must name a piece of evidence.

| Section | Points | Full marks when |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Decision rights | 10 | Every decision has a named role. Approval, operation and audit are separate roles. A tie-break rule exists with a deadline. Risk acceptance for Tier 3 sits with an accountable executive in writing. |
| 2. Tiers and routing | 10 | Decision times meet K1 for Tier 1 and Tier 2, and Tier 3 review is realistic. The design says how K2 is met without legal reviewing every use. |
| 3. Controls | 15 | Each tier has preventive, detective and corrective controls. Technical capabilities are cited as existing and with honest limits. Non-technical controls are present. No control claims reach a place the capability does not. |
| 4. Cadence | 10 | Each forum has a chair, members, interval, inputs and outputs. Internal audit (clause 9.2 style) and management review (clause 9.3 style) both appear with dates. |
| 5. Metrics | 10 | At least an activity, a value and a risk metric, each with source, owner, threshold and action. No metric is only a headline. |
| 6. Reporting | 5 | The Audit Committee gets a quarterly report with a defined content list. |
| 7. Conflict resolution | 10 | All five conflicts are resolved with the trade-off stated and a named accepter (2 points each). |
| 8. Evidence tie | 20 | Every commitment in the ledger cites evidence that exists or has a first-available date, and an owner who can produce it. Score is 20 times the share of commitments that qualify. A commitment with no evidence must be rewritten or dropped. |
| 9. Draft audit | 10 | Each of C1 to C10 has the right defect named and a repair (1 point each). |

## Scoring the evidence tie

A commitment qualifies when all of these are true:

1. It names at least one evidence ID or a described record.
2. The evidence would show the control working, not only that a rule exists.
3. It has a named owner role and a first-available date.
4. The evidence is dated.

Count the qualifying commitments, divide by the total, multiply by 20, round to the nearest point.

## Capacity check

If the program's committed hours exceed 87 a month and the student does not say what is cut or who is asked, take 3 points off section 2 and 3 points off section 8.

## Common scoring calls

- "The CISO will monitor" without a record that shows monitoring happened is not evidence.
- A policy page is not evidence of a control.
- A metric with no threshold and no action is a number, not a measure.
