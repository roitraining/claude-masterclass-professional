# Lab 3 Scoring Rubric (Take-Home, Self-Scoring)

Total 100 points. Score your own register, or swap with a colleague. The facilitator key is in the Lab 3 Facilitator note and is shared after the take-home is due.

## Part A: Each register row (7 points a row, 84 points)

| Item | Points | Full marks when |
|---|---|---|
| Owner | 1 | A named current role that exists in the brief. Not blank, not a team, not a person who left. |
| Tier | 2 | Matches the facilitator key, or differs and states a reason that uses the tier rules. Tier follows the highest-risk fact. |
| Control | 2 | Specific, fits the tier, and states its own limit. A capability is cited as existing, not configured. A control that cannot reach the use scores 0. |
| Evidence | 2 | An ID that would show the control working, with an owner who can produce it. If no ID fits, a clearly described dated record also earns full marks. "Policy" is not evidence. |

## Part B: Whole portfolio (16 points)

| Item | Points |
|---|---|
| Caught the four planted traps: a stale owner, a hidden data class, a use that changed, a consultation that has not happened (2 points each) | 8 |
| Ranked the top three risks with reasons that name who is harmed and why the control is weak | 4 |
| Set a register review date and a trigger for an earlier review | 4 |

## Bands

| Band | Score |
|---|---|
| Gold | 85 to 100 |
| Silver | 70 to 84 |
| Bronze | 50 to 69 |
| Not yet | Below 50 |

## Notes

- Do not mark a row down for a different reasonable tier if the reason is stated.
- Do not give credit for a control that depends on a setting you invented.
- A row whose evidence cannot be produced today needs a first-available date.
